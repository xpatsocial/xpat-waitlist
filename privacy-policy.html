Xpat LLC, 5900 Balcones Dr, Suite 100, Austin, TX 78731.

Contact for anything privacy-related: hello@xpat.social.

3. How we use it and our legal bases

  • To provide x/pat (contract): your account, profile, map, chats, messages, events, and showing your content to other users.
  • Safety and moderation (legitimate interests and legal obligations): checking content for harmful material, handling reports, enforcing our Terms, preventing abuse and complying with law.
  • Improving the app (consent): usage analytics and crash reports. Both are off until you turn them on, in the prompt when you first open x/pat or in Settings → Privacy & Safety, and you can turn either off at any time.
  • Location and notifications (consent): only with your device permission, which you can withdraw in your phone's settings at any time.

Automated moderation

Text and photos you post (spots, posts, chat and direct messages, profile photos) are checked automatically for content such as harassment, hate, sexual content, violence or self-harm, using our own filters and OpenAI's moderation service. Content that is flagged may be blocked or hidden, and you will be told why. A person reviews reports and appeals. We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you solely by automated means.

5. International transfers

We are based in the United States and our providers store data in the USA. If you use x/pat from the EU, UK, Thailand, Mexico or elsewhere, your data is transferred to the USA. We rely on our providers' data processing agreements and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (or equivalent safeguards) for these transfers. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS).

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and content: for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account in the app, your account, personal data and content are deleted immediately and permanently. Requests by email are completed within 7 days.
  • Analytics events: up to 12 months, then deleted. They are linked to your user ID, not your email; if you delete your account and want them gone sooner, email us.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Push tokens: removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Safety records: reports and moderation decisions may be kept for up to 1 year, and longer where the law requires (for example, material reported to NCMEC must be preserved for 1 year).
  • Backups: up to 30 days, then deleted.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Settings → Export My Data gives you a copy of your data (JSON).
  • Correct: edit your profile at any time.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete Account, or see xpat.social/delete-account. You can also delete individual posts and spots.
  • Analytics and crash reports: Settings → Privacy & Safety → Usage Analytics / Crash Reports.
  • Location and notifications: turn off in your phone's settings.
  • Block and report other users from their profile or content.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Thailand's PDPA, Mexico's LFPDPPP or US state laws such as California's CCPA), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority. Email hello@xpat.social and we will respond within 30 days. If we turn down a request, we'll explain why, and you can reply to ask us to look again.

We don't sell or share your data

We don't sell personal data, and we don't share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising, as those terms are used in US state laws such as California's CCPA and the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. We don't use advertising IDs or ad networks, and this website sets no cookies or trackers. That includes sensitive data such as precise location, which we use only with your permission and never sell.

Global Privacy Control: because there is no sale or targeted advertising to switch off, we treat every user and visitor as opted out, whether or not your browser or device sends a GPC signal. If that ever changes, we will honor GPC and update this policy first.

8. Adults only (18+)

x/pat is for adults. You must be at least 18 to create an account. Sign-up asks for your date of birth and blocks anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we close it and delete their data. If you believe a minor is using x/pat, contact hello@xpat.social.

See also our Child Safety Standards.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and row-level security in our database, and we limit who can access personal data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

10. Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the date at the top. For significant changes we will notify you in the app.
